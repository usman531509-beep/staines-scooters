> [!IMPORTANT]
> This project is deployed on Vercel (not Lovable). `vite.config.ts` switches the Nitro preset to `vercel` when `VERCEL` is set, and `vercel.json` holds the install/build commands and the cron schedule. Keep the main branch in a working state; avoid rewriting pushed history.

- The signed-in shell (shadcn `Sidebar` + `SidebarInset`) is mounted once by `AccountShellLayout` in `_authenticated/route.tsx`; a page's `<AccountShell title=…>` only registers its options with it, so navigating swaps the page content without remounting the sidebar, header or till toolbar. Pages without `AccountShell` (receipts, invoices) render bare.
- Render the browser-only administrator bottom navigation in `AccountShell` on every screen size (as on Hounslow), alongside the sidebar on large screens; the sidebar gets bottom padding so its footer stays above the bar. This keeps it consistent across admin pages while leaving installed-app navigation independent.
- The signed-in shell never renders the public website header; on the public website, signed-in staff still see the navbar, with a profile menu (Dashboard → /admin or /staff/pos, Log out) in place of "Login".
- Keep AccountShell and AppShell page workspaces full-width without outer horizontal gutters; shared shell sizing prevents individual signed-in pages reverting to a centred layout.
- Rental rent for admins other than the exempt original owner is charged as direct charges on that admin's own Stripe Connect account (stripe-connect.server.ts); the exempt owner keeps the built-in Stripe flow so existing payments are untouched.
- Motorbike finance maths lives in `src/lib/finance-calc.ts` (pure, integer pence) and is recomputed server-side in `finance.server.ts` on every save; the browser only previews, so stored schedules are always server-authoritative.
- Early settlement reads the global rebate setting on every server operation and passes it to the pure calculation; cached browser settings never authorise rebates.
- Reusable administrator signatures are private per-user records; explicit finance signing copies the saved image/name into the agreement and records a snapshot-linked audit action so later signature edits cannot alter executed documents.
- Finance conversation notes are separate staff-only records, not customer-visible finance transaction notes, so internal conversations do not leak into customer statements.
- Agreement PDFs are generated on demand in the browser from the full rendered document, including both signatures, without relying on a print popup.
- `finance_transactions` is append-only (trigger blocks update/delete); corrections must be new reversal rows so the audit trail stays intact.
- Finance staff permissions are enforced server-side via `assertFinance` in `src/lib/finance-perms.ts`; staff without a saved permissions row keep default access so existing staff aren't locked out.
- Finance accounts (P&L, ledger, cash flow) are derived from the append-only `finance_transactions` kinds at read time rather than a separate ledger table, so the audit trail stays the single source of truth.
- Phase-two finance actions use a shared confirmation dialog in FinancePhase2 before mutations, explaining actual record/payment/delivery effects without changing server behaviour.
- Stock/Inventory (/admin/inventory) is batch-based (stock_batches, FIFO) and its undo writes reversal movements plus a stock_movement_undos row, because stock_movements are immutable for audit.
- Expanded inventory rows derive batch order from the existing received-date/created-date ordering and pass the selected batch into the adjustment dialog, so displayed FIFO and adjustment targets stay aligned with inventory data.
- Stock action popups use a shared full-window accessible dialog with a fixed close control; product forms use a bounded scroll panel with a sticky header so closing stays available on short screens.
- Stock counts use an atomic authorised RPC and immutable movement-linked postings; approval and undo preserve financial exclusion, and main P&L derives count gains/losses and inventory financial value from those postings without inventing cash movements.
- Only staff, admins and owners sign in; there are no customer accounts, loyalty, payment plans, Dawat or customer reminder/push features, so new work must not add customer self-service pages.
- Customers can register on /register (name, phone, optional email; no password): /api/public/register adds them to `pos_customers` with `created_by` null, de-duplicated by phone, and the admin Customers list badges them "Website". It is a contact record only, not an account.
- Till customers are plain contact records (`pos_customers`: name, phone, optional email) linked to a sale via `sales.pos_customer_id` only so receipts and invoices show who bought; they are soft-deleted so past receipts keep the name, and must not grow logins or loyalty.
- Built-but-hidden modules are switched off in `src/lib/features.ts` (currently finance and Stripe online payments): gate navigation, routes, public links and outward-facing server actions on the flag instead of deleting the module, so it can be re-enabled.
- Customers only get a receipt when staff send one: after a till sale (and on the sale page) WhatsApp and Email buttons send the same receipt as the printed one, built from src/lib/receipt-doc.ts. Email sends it as the body plus a PDF; WhatsApp opens the customer's chat with the receipt written out and a signed read-only link (/api/public/receipt/<saleId>?k=…, src/lib/receipt-link.server.ts, HMAC of RECEIPT_LINK_SECRET or the service-role key) to view or download it. Nothing is emailed to customers automatically, and there are no reminders, invoices, marketing or automatic rental billing. Rental weeks are created when staff list or open rentals and payments are recorded manually. Supabase Auth also sends its own password-reset email.
- App email goes through `sendEmail` in src/lib/notify.server.ts, sent as the shop's Microsoft 365 mailbox: Microsoft Graph over HTTPS when MS_TENANT_ID/MS_CLIENT_ID/MS_CLIENT_SECRET are set (app registration with Mail.Send; MAIL_FROM picks the mailbox), otherwise SMTP (SMTP_HOST/PORT/USER/PASS). GoDaddy Airo blocks outbound SMTP ports, so Graph is the working path there. Staff notifications (website enquiry and website registration only; till sales are not emailed) go to NOTIFY_EMAIL, default support@stainesscooter.com. All sending is fire-and-forget: a missing setting or error is logged and never blocks the action. Import the module dynamically inside server handlers so it stays out of the browser bundle.
- Customer credit ("pay later") is staff-side only: a POS sale with payment_method "credit" needs a till customer, books the full total as revenue, stores what's owed in sales.credit_amount_pence and every payment (including any part paid at the till) as a credit_payments row; `addCreditPayment`/`refreshSaleCredit` (src/lib/credit-ledger.ts) keep sales.credit_paid_pence/credit_status in step (no DB trigger) and put cash payments into the open till as cash-in movements. Staff take later payments on /staff/credit; a sale with payments can't be switched off credit, and undoing one pays its cash back out of the till.
- Recurring (weekly/monthly) expenses are posted by `postDueRecurringExpenses` (src/lib/recurring-expenses.server.ts) whenever staff load Expenses, the Dashboard or P&L, because GoDaddy has no cron; each period is claimed by a conditional `next_run_date` update so concurrent loads can't double-post. The cron route remains for an external scheduler.
- Hosting is GoDaddy (Airo), whose env vars reach only the running server: the browser's Supabase URL and publishable key fall back to values the server writes into the page (src/lib/public-env.ts) when VITE_* weren't present at build time.
- Public contact-form submissions are stored in `contact_enquiries` by the server (service role) and followed up in /admin/enquiries; anonymous users have no direct table access.
- Every public enquiry (rent, buy, claim, repair, general, and the contact form) goes to the shop's WhatsApp via `whatsappLink`/`enquire` (src/lib/brand.ts, src/lib/scooters.ts); the contact form opens WhatsApp inside the submit gesture so it isn't popup-blocked, then saves its copy.
- Rental prices, deposit, minimum term, the sale line-up and the extras supplied with every sold scooter live in src/lib/scooters.ts; pages read them from there rather than hard-coding figures.
- Brand colours are yellow & black: admin tokens in src/styles.css (yellow `--primary` with black text; `--primary-ink` gold for primary text on white), public site in src/styles/landing.css (`ss-*` classes). Use the white logo (`staines-logo-light.webp`) on dark backgrounds.
